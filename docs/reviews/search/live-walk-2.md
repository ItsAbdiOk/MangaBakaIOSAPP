Device: iPhone 16 Pro simulator, UDID B963E62F-F657-4C7F-AF7A-CCF9E6C8DCF0, app `dev.abdirahmanmohamed.mangabaka` (new build, already launched, not rebuilt for this walk). Screenshots were viewed inline during the session but the screenshot tool used here does not expose a file path or write PNGs to disk (checked `/private/tmp`, `/var/folders` for anything the tool might have saved — nothing appeared), so unlike the first walk this session has no numbered PNG files to cite; findings below are described from the images actually seen at each step, in order, with an honest note wherever timing let something slip past. **The session was cut short by an environment failure** — see the note at the end — so steps 5 through 9 in the brief were not reached.

## 1. Idle screen, field focus, keyboard, scope bar

- Cold idle screen: large left-aligned "Search" title, a "Browse" (filled/active-looking) and "Surprise me" button pair, then straight into a "Filters" panel (Type, Status, Sort, Minimum rating, Year, Narrow by: Tags/Genres/Publishers, Offline toggle, Clear all / Show results), with a floating capsule search field pinned near the bottom ("Title, author, or tag" placeholder). **No "Recent" list is shown on this idle screen itself** — that's a change from the previous walk's doc, which described Recent search chips inline above the filter builder. Recents now only surface once you focus the field (see below).
- Tapping the field: the "Search" large title and the idle Filters panel are replaced by a **scope bar** (All · Manga · Manh(wa) · Manh(ua) · Novel · OEL, with "All" highlighted) and a plain list of 4 Recent searches (clock icon + red/orange text: "one piece", "blossoming blade", "berserk", "hunter x hunter"). This matches the brief's expected "recents as suggestions" pattern.
- The field itself shows a trailing **X (circle-x) icon** instead of the standard iOS "Cancel" text button. Functionally similar (closes/backs out of search) but visually different from a stock `UISearchBar`.
- No on-screen software keyboard was ever observed rendering in any screenshot taken during this walk, even with the field focused and a blinking text cursor visible. This matches the previous walk's note — either the simulator has a hardware keyboard toggle suppressing the software one, or screenshots are being taken between the keyboard's appearance and this tool's capture, or the keyboard genuinely never renders for this input path. Cannot distinguish with this tooling; same caveat as before.
- On a later re-focus of the field (after having already searched once), the scope bar did *not* reappear above the recents list — only the four recent rows were shown directly under the status bar. This looked like a one-off rendering inconsistency (scope bar present on first focus, absent on a later focus with an otherwise identical empty/focused state) rather than a clear repro; noting it as **worth checking**, not a confirmed bug, since I did not have time to isolate the exact trigger before the environment failure.

## 2. THE BIG ONE — Tags quick-pick from idle

**Not completed as scoped, and this is itself the most important finding of the walk.**

After scrolling the idle Filters panel down to reveal the "Narrow by: Tags / Genres / Publishers" row, tapping **Tags** produced no visible response — no sheet opened. I retried the identical tap five times (including a deliberate double-tap) across roughly a minute of wall-clock time; no sheet ever appeared. I then tried **Genres** at the same scroll position — also no response. I tried the highly visible, full-width **"Show results"** button and the **"Any"** rating chip at that same scroll position — neither responded either, even though "Show results" is about as large and unambiguous a tap target as exists on the screen.

This was **not** a general app freeze: immediately after, tapping the search field itself (a different element, same screen) worked instantly (it focused and showed Recent suggestions). Scrolling also worked throughout — I could scroll the Filters panel up and down freely while none of Tags/Genres/Any/Show results responded to taps.

So the reproducible shape of the bug, as observed, is: **after scrolling the idle Filters panel, several of its own controls (Tags, Genres, the rating chips, and the Show-results button) stop responding to taps, while the search field and scroll gestures on the same screen keep working.** I could not isolate whether this is scroll-position-dependent, a hit-testing/z-order issue from a sticky header, or a stale gesture recognizer left over from the scroll — I did not have `inspect` (accessibility tree) available to check what was actually receiving the touches, and the environment failure (see below) cut off further isolation before I could retry from a fresh, non-scrolled idle screen.

**I was not able to reach the Tags sheet at all this session**, so the specific regression question the brief asked about — whether picking a tag and closing the sheet now keeps the idle panel alive (with a token/count) instead of dead-ending on "Nothing matched" — is **unverified**. I can't confirm the old bug is fixed, and I can't confirm it still exists; I simply couldn't get the entry point to respond.

One partial, accidental data point: earlier in the walk, a mis-tap on the scrolled Filters panel landed on the "7+" minimum-rating chip instead of a scroll, and that one *did* register normally (highlighted "Any/6+/7+" and updated "Show results" to "Show 27,366 results" live) — so filter chips are not universally broken, only Tags/Genres/Show-results/Any were unresponsive in the specific sequence tried.
