## Description (4,000)

MangaBaka is a manga discovery app for readers who have run out of things
to read.

FIND SOMETHING
• Discover — what's rising this week, hidden gems, and what's trending.
• Stack — swipe through covers; save the ones worth a look, skip the rest.
• Mix — pick series you love and get a blend built from them.
• Search — by title, author, tag, genre or publisher; browse the tag tree.

KNOW WHAT'S COMING
• Release estimates for series you follow, from their actual release history.
• Announced volumes with dates, and the next volume on your Home Screen.
• "Due this week" and "Pick back up" widgets, and Siri: "What's out this
  week in MangaBaka?"

EVERY VOLUME ON RECORD
• Volumes grouped by edition and language, with ISBNs and dates, from
  Anime News Network, Open Library and Japan's National Diet Library.
• Tick the volumes you own; see what's missing. Scan a barcode in the shop
  to check.
• The same story in other formats — the light novel behind the manga, the
  manga behind the anime.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Paste a MangaBaka personal access token and your library, reading
  progress and ratings appear here, with recommendations built from what
  you've actually read.
• Share a link from AniList, MyAnimeList or MangaUpdates to the app and
  land on the series.

HONEST BY DESIGN
• No account needed. Discover, Stack, Mix and Search work signed out.
• No ads, no purchases, no tracking. Nothing about you leaves the app.
• Content is filtered to safe and suggestive by default; stronger content
  is behind a deliberate opt-in in Settings.
• Open source.

Series data from MangaBaka (mangabaka.org), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Volume records
from Anime News Network, Open Library, National Diet Library Search and
Wikidata, credited in the app.
