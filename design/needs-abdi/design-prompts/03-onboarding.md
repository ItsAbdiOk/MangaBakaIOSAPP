# First run

**Status:** three pages I wrote as a placeholder, explicitly to be replaced.
Built only from things the app can actually do, so a redesign has real
boundaries.

## The one decision already made, and worth keeping

**It is not a gate.** Every discovery endpoint answers without a token, so a new
reader gets a fully working app with no account. Nothing stands between opening
the app and seeing covers. Onboarding explains what an account *adds* and then
gets out of the way.

## What it can honestly promise

- A daily stack of series to swipe through, drawn from what is rising
- Search across everything, with filters and saved lenses
- A mix: pick up to three series you like, get a blend explained by the tags
  they share
- A library with reading states and ratings
- A release schedule for what you are reading

Only the last three need an account, and only for *your* data.

## Where it has to hand off

- "Connect an account" → Settings
- "Not now" → the app

## What to hand back

Three to four screens maximum. This is the one place a reader will not
tolerate length.
